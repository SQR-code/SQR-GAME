Rules
  1. Each player has eight tokens: two each of 1, 2, 3 and 4 dots. Black goes first.
  2. Placement: take turns placing any token from your supply on an empty point. Black can’t use the centre on the very first turn.
  3. Win by having four of your tokens on the corners of a square, any size, with sides along the grid lines. The numbers don’t matter for a win.
  4. If all 16 tokens are down with no square, movement begins in a fixed order: Black moves a 1, White a 1, Black a 2, White a 2, and so on up to 4, then back to 1. You choose which of your two tokens of that number to move, to any empty point.
  5. It’s a draw if the whole board layout repeats, or after 30 turns each (8 placing and 22 moving).
